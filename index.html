The Bauhaus

A German art school that ran for fourteen years, was shut down by politics, and still decides what most of the things around you look like.

Below is the Dessau building, designed by Walter Gropius in 1925. Steel frame, glass curtain wall, and the name set vertically in sans serif down the corner.

1919   Founded to Weimar

1925   Moved to Dessau

1930   Moved to Berlin

1933   Closed

The Bauhaus was founded in 1919 in the city of Weimar by German architect Walter Gropius in 1883-1969.

Gropius explained the vision for a union of art design in the Proclamation of the Bauhaus.

Gropius developed a craft-based curriculum that would turn out artisans and designers capable of creating useful and beautiful objects appropriate to this new system of living.

What came out of the workshops?

Furniture

Marcel Breuer ran the cabinetmaking workshop from 1924 to 1928 and spent it trying to reduce a chair to almost nothing. He thought chairs would eventually be replaced by columns of air. Looking at the steel tubes on his bicycle, he started bending metal instead of carving wood, and ended up with light chairs a factory could produce.
